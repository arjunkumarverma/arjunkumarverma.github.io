Executive Background & Philosophy

Bridging Scientific Rigor with Grassroots Governance

Over three decades of institutional leadership synthesizing empirical ecology with nationwide citizen mobilization.

Dr. Arjun Kumar Verma holds a Ph.D. in Botany with a specialized doctoral thesis on the "Flora and Vegetation of South Sikkim Himalaya" conducted under the guidance of Dr. M. P. Nayar, former Director of the Botanical Survey of India. Throughout his 33-year career with Nehru Yuva Kendra Sangathan (NYKS)—an autonomous statutory organization under the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India—he systematically harmonized scientific observation with mass grassroots mobilization.

Rising from frontline District Youth Coordinator roles across Sikkim, Odisha, and Bihar to State Director in Haryana and Himachal Pradesh, and Deputy Director at National Headquarters in New Delhi, Dr. Verma has steered cornerstone national programs. His responsibilities encompassed guiding 6,000+ village youth clubs, directing nationwide recruitments, formulating annual action plans for over 500 districts, and executing large-scale social defense programs funded by UNFPA and the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment.

Following his government service, Dr. Verma served as Director of Planning at Shobhit University (Meerut), architecting academic policy frameworks and pioneering Smart Tribal Farming models. As an IICA Certified Independent Director and Member of the IUCN Commission on Education and Communication, he actively counsels corporate boards, higher educational institutions, and civil society on transparent governance and sustainability.

Doctoral Dissertation

"Flora and Vegetation of South Sikkim Himalaya"

Supervised by: Dr. M. P. Nayar (Former Director, Botanical Survey of India)
Institution B. R. Ambedkar Bihar University
Conferred 2005 (Doctorate in Botany)
Geographic Zone South Sikkim Eastern Himalaya
Field Herbarium Botanical Survey of India Records

The research provides a comprehensive floristic enumeration, altitudinal vegetation zonation, and ecological documentation of South Sikkim. It details endemic angiosperms, sacred groves preserved by indigenous Lepcha communities, and anthropogenic pressures impacting fragile alpine and temperate forest ecosystems.

Peer-Reviewed Papers & Presentations

Published Contributions

  • Diversity of Plant Species of Sikkim Himalaya — Documenting rare and endangered vascular plants across varying altitudinal gradients.
  • Effect of Some Pollutant Gases on Some Crop Plants — Empirical research on industrial sulfur dioxide and nitrous oxide atmospheric impacts on agrarian yields.
  • Rhododendron of Sikkim Himalaya — Taxonomic distribution and ecological threats facing Ericaceae taxa in Eastern Himalayan valleys.
  • Climbing Taxa of Sikkim Himalaya — Morphological classifications and canopy ecology of lianas and climbing species.
  • Sacred Groves of Sikkim Himalaya — Research presented at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bangalore under Prof. Madhav Gadgil.
